Fixed: a day could end up with two entries. Tapping + or the daily reminder
now reopens today's entry instead of starting a second one.

Fixed: WebDAV sync over plain http:// was blocked by Android and reported as a
generic connection error. Self-hosted servers on a LAN work again.

Fixed: entries started just after midnight were filed under the previous day.

Security: WebDAV entries are now authenticated with HMAC-SHA256, so a malicious
server can no longer alter them unnoticed. Existing entries stay readable and
are upgraded when you next save them. The settings screen warns when a http://
server is used, because WebDAV credentials are not encrypted in transit.

Removed 20 launcher-badge permissions that came from a notification library.
SimpleDay never sets a badge.
